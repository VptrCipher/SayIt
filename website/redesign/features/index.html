Features

Your voice, in context.

Explore how SayIt turns speech into text in the app you’re using.

01 / Dictation

Hold. Speak. Release.

A configurable hotkey starts recording. Sherpa-ONNX transcribes on your device after you release, and SayIt inserts the text into the active editable field. Escape cancels recording or transcription.

Try the formatting demo →

02 / Context

Formatting follows the app.

Process and window metadata help choose a Developer, Email, Chat, Notes, Prompt, or Normal profile. Manual selection takes priority.

03 / Local rules

Put words in the right form.

Technical terms
Format clearly spoken URLs, paths, and identifiers. “Get user by ID, camel case” becomes getUserById.
Voice edits
Say “scratch that”, replace a phrase, or delete the last sentence.
Structure
Use spoken commands for line breaks, paragraphs, and lists.
Snippets and vocabulary
Map a spoken phrase to saved text or a spelling. Snippets insert literal text. You choose which entries and remembered corrections stay enabled.
Safe zones
Block recording in the apps you mark as protected.

What to expect.

Dictation needs an installed model. Speed varies by hardware, model, and utterance length. Text insertion requires a focused editable field, and clipboard contents may be replaced. Linux support is partial.
